# ShopEase Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: How do I track my order?**
Provide your order ID (e.g., ORD1001) and we will look up the tracking number and delivery status.

**Q2: What is the return window?**
General items: 30 days from delivery. Electronics: 14 days. Food/perishables: non-returnable unless damaged.

**Q3: How long do refunds take?**
5-7 business days after we receive the return, plus bank processing time.

**Q4: Is there free shipping?**
Yes, free standard shipping on all orders over ₹499. Orders under ₹499 incur a flat ₹49 fee.

**Q5: Can I cancel my order?**
Only if status is "processing". Shipped/delivered orders must be returned instead.

**Q6: What if my item is out of stock?**
We will notify you and suggest alternatives or a refund. Out-of-stock items show stock=0 in catalog.

**Q7: Do products have warranties?**
Electronics: 1-year (TVs 2-year). Apparel/grocery: no warranty, but returnable per policy.

**Q8: How do I initiate a return?**
Contact support with order ID and product ID. We verify eligibility and issue a return label.

**Q9: Do you ship internationally?**
Yes, to select international destinations with delivery in 7-14 business days (fees calculated at checkout).

**Q10: How is my payment data handled?**
Encrypted via TLS, never stored in full, and processed securely via UPI and RBI-compliant gateways.
