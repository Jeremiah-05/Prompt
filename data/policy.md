# ShopEase Store Policy

## 1. Shipping Policy
We offer free standard shipping on all orders over ₹499. Orders under ₹499 incur a flat ₹49 shipping fee. Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days across India. Express shipping (1-2 business days) is available for an additional ₹149. International shipping is available to select countries with delivery in 7-14 business days and fees calculated at checkout. Orders are processed within 1 business day. Tracking information is emailed once the order ships.

## 2. Return Policy — General
All non-food, non-perishable items may be returned within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund. Items must be in original condition and packaging. To initiate a return, contact support with your order ID. Once approved, you will receive a prepaid return label via email. Refunds are issued within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item. Return shipping is free for defective items; otherwise a ₹79 deduction applies.

## 3. Return Policy — Electronics Exception
**Electronics** (laptops, phones, TVs, cameras, gaming consoles, smart watches, headphones) have a shorter return window of **14 days** from delivery. This shorter window exists due to rapid depreciation and hygiene concerns. Electronics must be unopened or in like-new condition with all accessories. If the electronics item is defective, the 14-day limit is waived and a replacement or refund will be issued under warranty. Grocery and perishable food items (tea, coffee, chocolate) are **non-returnable** unless damaged during shipping — report within 48 hours with photos.

## 4. Refund Timeline
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after the returned item is received at our warehouse. Refunds go to the original payment method. For credit/debit card and UPI refunds, allow an additional 3-5 business days for your bank to post the credit. Net banking and wallet refunds are issued once processed. You will receive an email confirmation when the refund is initiated.

## 5. Warranty Policy
Electronics carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty (2 years for TVs). Apparel has no warranty but follows the 30-day return policy. Defective items under warranty are replaced free of charge — contact support with order ID, product ID, and description of the defect. Warranty does not cover accidental damage, misuse, or normal wear.

## 6. Payment & Privacy
We accept UPI, credit cards, debit cards, and Net Banking. All transactions are encrypted via TLS. We never store full card numbers or UPI PINs. For order inquiries, we verify identity via order ID. We do not share customer data with third parties. Promotional emails can be unsubscribed at any time.

## 7. Order Cancellation
Orders in "processing" status can be cancelled for a full refund before they ship. Once an order is "shipped" or "delivered" it cannot be cancelled — use the return process instead.

## 8. Customer Support Hours & Escalation
Our AI assistant handles queries 24/7. For complex issues (payment disputes, bulk orders, legal), the bot will escalate to a human agent within 24 hours. Human support hours are Mon-Fri 9am-6pm IST.
